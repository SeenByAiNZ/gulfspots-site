01What Gulf Spots does

Gulf Spots suggests fishing spots in the Hauraki Gulf for the trip you describe (where you launch, the day and time, how far you’ll travel and your boat). It scores spots using conditions such as wind, tide, light and season, shows how to fish each spot, and points you to it using your phone’s GPS.

Spots are good places to try. We don’t promise you’ll catch fish, or that a spot, its depth or its description is accurate or up to date. Forecasts can be wrong and conditions change quickly.

02Safety on the water

  • Use your chart plotter and official charts to navigate, and keep a proper lookout. Don’t rely on Gulf Spots to avoid rocks, reefs, shallows, other vessels or hazards.
  • Check the official marine forecast and conditions before you leave and while you’re out. The wind limits in the app are a guide only: your boat, your experience and the sea state decide what’s safe.
  • Follow Maritime New Zealand and local boating rules, and carry the safety gear the law requires.
  • Phone GPS can be out by tens of metres or more, and phones can lose power or signal.

03Fishing rules and protected areas

You must follow the fishing rules, including size limits, daily limits, closed areas and method rules, and the rules for marine reserves, cable zones and other protected areas. We never recommend spots inside marine reserves, and we mark some areas with extra warnings, but those marks aren’t complete or official. Check the current rules with Fisheries New Zealand before every trip.
