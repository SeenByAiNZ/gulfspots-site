Delete your account

Gulf Spots · Northbeam Limited · 18 October 2026

You can delete your Gulf Spots account, and everything linked to it, at any time. It can't be undone.

Subscribed? Deleting your account doesn't cancel the subscription. Cancel it first in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings.

01In the app

  • Open Gulf Spots and go to Saved.
  • Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted from our server straight away.

02Without the app

Lost your phone or removed the app? Email hello@gulfspots.co.nz from the email address on your account, or include your Tester ID from the app's Saved screen. We'll confirm it's your account, delete it and reply within 20 working days.

03What's deleted

  • Your account ID and backup email address.
  • Spots you added, your favourites and your private catch ratings.
  • Any spot requests you sent us.

04What's kept

  • Purchase records are held by Google or Apple under their own policies. We can't delete them.
  • Short-term technical logs kept by our service providers (such as IP address and time) expire on their own, within a few weeks.
  • Combined, anonymised rating totals that can't be traced back to you.
  • A beta sign-up made on this website is separate. Ask us at the same address and we'll remove it too.
