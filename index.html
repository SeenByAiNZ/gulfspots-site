The spot is half of it. The day is the rest.

A reef that fires on a north-east breeze and a running tide can be dead on a southerly at slack water. Gulf Spots scores every spot in two-hour windows for the next two weeks, so you know where to go and when.

Tide

Fish feed when the water moves. Each spot is scored on how hard the tide runs over it, and which way.
