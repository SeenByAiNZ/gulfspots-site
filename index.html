Go to spot

  • Plan by trip

    Pick where you launch, the day, how far you’ll travel and your boat. Gulf Spots scores two weeks ahead and shows the best time in small print, so you choose.

  • How to fish it

    For each spot: where to anchor, the method, rig, bait and berley, and what to do if it’s quiet.

  • Chart and GPS

    See your spots on the chart, then the app shows distance and bearing from your phone until you’re on the spot.

  • Your own spots

    Add the spots you already fish. They stay private to you, and the AI scores them for each trip alongside ours.

  • Wind limits

    Set your boat size and any window over its wind limit is closed. Wind is forecast; tide, light and season are known two weeks out.

  • Works offline

    Save the Gulf chart and your trip before you leave. On the water you don’t need signal to find the spot.

QuestionsBefore you sign up

Good to know.

Will it find me fish every time?

No app can. Gulf Spots picks the places with the best chance for your trip and tells you why. The fish still get a say.

Does it work without signal?

You need signal to plan a trip. Save the Gulf chart and your trip before you go, and finding the spot on the water works without coverage.

Can other people see my spots?

No. Your own spots, favourites and ratings are private to your account. We never share them or show them to other users.

Is it a navigation app?

No. It shows distance and bearing to a spot, never a route or course. Use your chart plotter and official charts, and keep a lookout.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and Android phones and tablets. During the beta, Android testers join through Google Play and iPhone testers through TestFlight.

What about size and bag limits?

Always check the current rules before you go. Fisheries New Zealand lists them for the Auckland and Kermadec area, including the free fishing rules app: mpi.govt.nz.

How do I cancel?

In your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. You keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
