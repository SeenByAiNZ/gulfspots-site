Gulf Spots

Privacy policy

Version 2 · 11 November 2026 · Northbeam Limited

This policy explains what personal information Gulf Spots collects, why, and what you can do about it. It follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Gulf Spots is made by Northbeam Limited (“we”, “us”).

The short version: we collect as little as we can and never sell your data. We do measure which of our ads bring people to Gulf Spots; on iPhone that uses your advertising identifier only if you tap Allow when the app asks. Your position, trips, spots and ratings are never used for advertising, and your own spots and ratings are private to you. You can see, correct or delete your information at any time.

01What we collect

  • Your account. A random account ID made when you first open the app. If you back up your account, your email address.
  • Your trips. The launch point, day, distance and boat size you choose, sent to our server to score spots for that trip.
  • Your position. Only when you ask for it: to set your launch point, to save a spot you’re on, or to show distance and bearing to a spot. Distance and bearing are worked out on your phone; your live position isn’t sent to us. We don’t track your position in the background.
  • Your spots, favourites and ratings. Spots you add (name, position, type, depth), spots you star, and your private catch ratings.
  • Your subscription. Whether you have an active subscription or trial, from Apple or Google. We never get your card details.
  • Beta sign-ups on this website. Your email, phone type and, if you give it, where you usually launch.
  • Beta testing. When you join the beta we email you a personal access code. When you use it in the app, your sign-up is linked to your app account, so we know you’ve started and can send setup help and the survey. Your survey answers are linked to your sign-up so we can give you the tester discount.
  • Website visits. We count visits to this website with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies: the page, the site or ad you came from, your browser type and country. It doesn’t identify you or follow you to other sites.
  • Ad measurement. When you open the app, the Meta (Facebook) software in it records that Gulf Spots was installed and opened, with technical details of your phone. On iPhone it includes your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking; on Android it includes your advertising ID unless you have turned it off in your phone’s settings. When you start a trial or subscription, our subscription provider tells Meta and Apple Ads that it happened (not your name or email), so we can tell which ads work.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, a token that lets us send them to your phone.
  • Where you heard about us. Your answer, if you choose to give one.
  • A phone identifier for the free look. A random code (iPhone) or a code made from your phone’s Android ID, so the free first look at spots is once per phone.
  • Technical records. Our service providers keep short-term logs (such as IP address, time and the request) to run and protect the service.

02Why we use it

  • To run Gulf Spots: score spots for your trips, keep your saved spots, and let you restore your account on a new phone.
  • To send the emails you ask for: backup and restore codes and, if you signed up for the beta, your access code, setup reminders, the survey, your tester discount and app news. Every beta email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To improve Gulf Spots: we read survey answers and combine them with spot ratings to decide what to fix.
  • To protect our spot data: spots sent to your phone are tied to your account so we can trace copies.
  • To improve rankings, using ratings in anonymised, combined form.
  • To measure which of our ads and other marketing bring people to Gulf Spots, and to send notifications you have allowed (such as good fishing conditions coming up).
  • To see how many people visit this website and where they came from, as counts only.

We don’t sell your information or share your spots with other users. We don’t use your position, trips, spots or ratings for advertising.

03Who helps us run Gulf Spots

We use these providers to store or process information for us. Most are overseas, mainly in Australia and the United States, so your information may be held outside New Zealand. We choose providers that protect it to a standard comparable to New Zealand law.

  • Database and accounts: hosted in Sydney, Australia.
  • Email: a provider that sends backup, restore and beta emails.
  • Subscriptions: a provider that keeps track of subscription status and reports trials and subscriptions to Meta and Apple Ads for ad measurement.
  • App updates and notifications: a provider that delivers app updates and notifications (on Android through Google).
  • Maps: map tiles from the New Zealand government’s basemap service. Your phone downloads them directly, so the service sees your IP address and the map area shown.
  • Website: hosting, fonts and visit counts (no cookies) for this website, and delivery of the offline chart to the app. These see your IP address when a page or the chart loads.
  • Apple and Google: app stores, subscriptions and payments, under their own privacy policies.
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram): ad measurement, in the United States and elsewhere, under Meta’s privacy policy.
  • Apple Ads: measures installs and subscriptions from our ads in the App Store.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

04How long we keep it

  • Account information, spots, favourites and ratings: until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • Trip requests: kept only as long as needed to score and show the trip, and in short-term logs.
  • Your last trip: a copy is kept on your phone so you can still see it with no signal, for up to 3 days. It never leaves the phone, is kept separately for each account, is replaced by your next trip, and is removed when you delete the app.
  • Beta sign-ups and survey answers: until six months after public launch, unless you become a subscriber or ask us to remove you sooner.
  • Notification tokens, your answer on where you heard about us, and the free-look record: until you delete your account.

05Keeping it safe

Information is encrypted in transit. Each account can reach only its own spots, favourites and ratings. Access to our systems is limited and protected with two-factor sign-in.

06Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete your account and everything linked to it. Email hello@gulfspots.co.nz from the address on your account, or include your Tester ID from the app’s Saved screen. We’ll reply within 20 working days. Steps to delete your account are on Delete your account.

You can turn tracking off at any time in your iPhone’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android phone’s settings. You can turn notifications off in your phone’s settings for Gulf Spots.

If you’re not happy with how we handle your information, tell us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

07Children

Gulf Spots isn’t aimed at children under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their information without a parent or guardian’s permission.

08Changes

We’ll update this policy when what we collect or how we use it changes, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
